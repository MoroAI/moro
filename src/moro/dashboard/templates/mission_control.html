MoroAI Foundry Engineering Handbook

                    
Operational manual for local-first adaptation, privacy governance, and continuous alignment.

                

                
                

                    

                        
1. The Epistemic Compiler

                        
Supported conversation records are normalized and scored with compression, glossary and rarity heuristics. Raw documents require explicit question-and-answer preparation. Review filtering decisions before training.

                    

                    

                        
2. Catastrophic Forgetting Auto-Ratio Formula

                        
Mixture suggestions are heuristic. Replay and KL regularization are not implemented in the trainer; use held-out evaluations to measure regression.

                    

                    

                        
3. Reviewed feedback extraction

                        
The webhook records feedback for extraction. Explicit human corrections are used by default; synthetic heuristics require opt-in. Review preferences before training. An autonomous DPO trainer is not implemented.
