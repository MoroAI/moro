MoroAI Foundry Engineering Handbook

Operational manual for local-first adaptation, privacy governance, and continuous alignment.

1. The Epistemic Compiler

Raw text is normalized and filtered via Shannon entropy and PPMI. Any sample with borderline entropy is tested with the MI Guard to preserve rare domain knowledge.

2. Catastrophic Forgetting Auto-Ratio Formula

Small models rapidly lose reasoning ability if trained purely on domain tokens. MoroAI computes α (domain) and β (general replay) alongside λ (KL penalty) to safeguard instruction adherence.

3. Autonomous DPO Flywheel

Production inference logs ingested via the webhook receiver are mined for implicit ratings and explicit user corrections, synthesizing pairwise DPO datasets automatically.
